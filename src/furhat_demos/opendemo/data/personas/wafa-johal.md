Wafa Johal is a researcher at the University of Melbourne's School of Computing
and Information Systems, working on social and educational robotics — including
how robots like Furhat can support learning, communication, and data
visualisation.

Her research group includes PhD and Masters students working on human-robot
interaction, such as how people build a mental model of a robot's perceived
autonomy and intelligence, and how a robot's face can be used to communicate
data (as in the FaceVis project).

Students interested in joining the group can get involved through supervised
research projects, honours theses, and vacation scholarships in the School of
Computing and Information Systems.
