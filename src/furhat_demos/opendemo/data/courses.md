The School of Computing and Information Systems offers several courses. The Bachelor
of Science has a major in Computing, covering software engineering, algorithms,
artificial intelligence, and data science. Students choose elective courses from
second year onwards.

The Master of Information Technology is a two-year graduate program for students from
any undergraduate background, with specialisations including Artificial Intelligence,
Computing, Distributed Computing, and Data Science.

The Bachelor of Design offers a major in Computing and Software Systems for students
interested in combining design thinking with software development.
