Our teaching approach emphasises project-based learning: most subjects include a
substantial group project, and final-year students complete an industry capstone
project working with a real external partner organisation.

Class sizes are kept small in later years, with workshops and studio-style teaching
replacing large lectures once students specialise.

Assessment mixes traditional exams with continuous project work, code reviews, and
presentations, reflecting how software is actually built in industry.
