The School of Computing and Information Systems hosts research groups spanning human-
computer interaction, artificial intelligence, computer vision, distributed systems,
and software engineering.

Wafa Johal leads a research group working on social and educational robotics, including
how robots like Furhat can support learning, communication, and data visualisation.
Her group includes PhD and Masters students working on human-robot interaction.

Undergraduate and Masters students can get involved in research through supervised
research projects, honours theses, and vacation scholarships.
